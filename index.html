VideoBrain Publishing is an operator-controlled tool for publishing the operator's approved videos and recording their publication results. It is not a public account-connection service and has no public sign-up.

What the YouTube connection does

  • Checks the authorized channel's identity before publishing.
  • Uploads approved videos, applies supported publishing settings and cover artwork, and schedules supported releases.
  • Reads the selected videos' publication status and records native video identifiers and timestamps.

The wider private workflow can publish to other selected social platforms and analyze the operator's performance data. Each platform has its own authorization and publishing requirements.

Account control

The operator authorizes Google access through Google's own consent screen. This public website does not request Google credentials, receive OAuth callbacks, store access tokens, or upload videos. Google access can be revoked from Google Account connections.

Policies and support

Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. For authorization questions, use the support contact shown on the Google consent screen. This site does not provide account access to outside users.
