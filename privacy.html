Last updated: November 17, 2026. This policy describes the Google/YouTube connection and related operator-controlled publishing workflow. VideoBrain Publishing is a personal-use tool, not a public account-connection service.

Data accessed and why

With the operator's Google authorization, the tool accesses the authorized YouTube channel's identity and selected video metadata, publication settings and status. It sends approved video files, titles, descriptions, cover artwork and publishing settings to YouTube when instructed. These data are used to verify the destination, publish or schedule approved content, and confirm the resulting publication.

The current Google authorization requests the youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and youtube.force-ssl scopes. The last scope grants broader YouTube management capabilities than uploading alone; this integration uses it to update an approved video's visibility or schedule after applying its cover. It does not request Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar or Contacts access. Google explains the requested permissions on its consent screen.

Storage and retention

OAuth access and refresh credentials are stored in the operator's private local configuration. Publishing records, approved inputs, publication evidence and analysis outputs are stored in the operator's private workspace. They are not placed on this public website. The public website does not receive or store OAuth credentials.

Publication records and analysis evidence can be retained for later comparison. The operator controls local retention. An explicitly enrolled automatic workflow may delete its source/editing working folders after complete verified analysis and safety checks, while retaining final videos, publication records and analysis evidence.

Service providers and sharing

Publishing sends approved content to the selected platform. Where explicitly authorized, the wider workflow can use publishing or media-transfer providers such as Buffer and Cloudinary. Those providers may retain approved media under their own policies; automatic remote-media deletion is not promised.

Authenticated GitHub Copilot tooling can assist with publishable wording and operator-requested performance analysis. OAuth credentials are not intentionally included in those review or analysis inputs. The integration does not implement the training of general-purpose AI models.

The tool does not sell Google account data. Its purpose is the operator's requested publishing, verification and analysis. VideoBrain Publishing's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion

The operator can revoke the connection at any time using Google Account connections, and can remove the associated local credential files and stored records from the private workspace. Revocation does not automatically delete local records or videos already published to YouTube; published videos are managed separately in YouTube Studio.

This website

These are static information pages hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no sign-in form, advertising code or application analytics scripts. GitHub may process visitor connection logs under its privacy statement.

Platform policies and contact

Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

For authorization or data questions, use the support contact shown on the Google consent screen. The application is operated for personal use and does not accept public user registrations.
